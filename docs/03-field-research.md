# field-research

**Status:** Discovery-phase. Based on direct, on-the-ground travel experience across Spiti and Zanskar and conversations along the way — not a formal survey. See [`01-founder-story.md`](01-founder-story.md) for the full narrative.

## Observations

- Many homestays have no online presence at all.
- Hosts rely on word of mouth and WhatsApp, not listings or booking platforms.
- Travellers default to asking local shops and tea stalls for recommendations — that channel already works, it just isn't captured anywhere.
- Clean toilets are hard to locate.
- Mobile network coverage varies significantly by operator (e.g. one carrier works where another doesn't), and this is rarely documented anywhere.
- Mechanics are known locally but effectively invisible online.
- Travel information relevant to a single trip is fragmented across many unrelated platforms.

## Learnings

- Travellers are more likely to contribute information *while travelling* than after they return home — the contribution window is narrow and in-the-moment.
- Local hosts are generally comfortable sharing basic information: location, photos, phone number. Onboarding friction for hosts is practical, not a privacy objection.
- Given enough factual detail and photographs, travellers trust structured facts over star ratings. This is the basis for D-004 (facts over ratings) in [`14-decision-log.md`](14-decision-log.md).
