# users

Lamyig initially serves three groups.

## Primary users — independent budget travellers

Solo riders, backpackers, trekkers, budget travellers. People who need in-the-moment, practical information (fuel, water, network, mechanics, homestays) more often than they need a place to book.

## Secondary users — local service providers

Homestays, campsites, cafés, mechanics, guides. People who already deliver a good service but have no realistic way to be found online, and no interest in fighting for visibility inside a commission-driven booking platform.

## Contributors

Travellers, riders, trekkers, local residents, travel creators — anyone who adds or verifies information. Contribution is the mechanism that keeps the knowledge base alive; see [`08-mvp.md`](08-mvp.md) for the contribution flow.
